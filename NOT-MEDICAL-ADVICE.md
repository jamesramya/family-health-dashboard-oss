# Not Medical Advice

**This software is a personal record-keeping tool. It is not a medical device, not a diagnostic tool, and does not provide medical advice.**

## What this means for you

- **Reference ranges shown in the app are approximate.** They are sourced from publicly available guidelines (NHS, WHO, RCPA, and others) and may not match your lab's specific ranges, your personal health profile, your age, your sex, or your clinical situation. Always refer to the ranges printed on your actual lab report.

- **AI-assisted extraction can be wrong.** Documents are processed by AI models (Gemini, Claude). The extracted values and labels may contain errors, especially for unusual test names, handwritten notes, or low-quality scans. Always verify important values against the source document.

- **Trends do not replace clinical judgment.** Seeing a number go up or down is not the same as understanding whether it matters for your health. Interpretation of lab results requires clinical context, history, and professional training.

- **Do not use this app to make medical decisions.** Do not start, stop, or change any medication or treatment based on information shown here. Always consult a qualified doctor, nurse practitioner, or other licensed healthcare professional.
